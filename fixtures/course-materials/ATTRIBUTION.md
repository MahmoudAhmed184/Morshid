## Modifications made

These PDFs are adaptations of the upstream work, not verbatim copies:

- The upstream PDF/print export of the book (retrieved 2026-07-09) was split into five grouped part PDFs using `pypdf`. The split was performed on page boundaries, so some parts carry over the tail of the preceding chapter.
- Chapter ranges: Part 1 = chapters 3-5; Part 2 = chapters 6-8; Part 3 = chapters 10-12; Part 4 = chapters 13-15; Part 5 = chapters 16-17.

No content was otherwise rewritten; the modifications are limited to selection, splitting, and omission as described above.
